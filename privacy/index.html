Privacy Policy

Last updated 17 October 2026

This policy explains how Speedrun AI Labs Pty Ltd (ABN 38 695 878 356) (“Speedrun AI Labs”, “we”, “us”) handles personal information when you use speedrunlab.ai or contact us. We handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles.

What we collect

When you send the contact form, we collect your name, email address, subject, phone number if you give one, your message and the time you sent it. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you include.

Like most websites, our hosting provider records technical information about each request, such as your IP address and browser type. To keep spam out, your IP address is checked by Cloudflare Turnstile when you send the form. Our own logs store a one-way code made from your email address, not the address itself.

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and talk with you about our agents.
  • To protect the website and the contact form from spam and abuse.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use advertising or analytics cookies on this website.

Who we share it with

We use a small number of service providers to run this website: Vercel (hosting), Cloudflare (Turnstile spam protection) and Resend (delivering contact form messages to our inbox). They may store or process information outside Australia, including in the United States. We share only what each service needs to do its job.

Agents we deploy for businesses

When we deploy Anton or Joseph for a business, how that business’s data is handled is set out in our agreement with it. Where an agent runs on the business’s own hardware or in its own environment, the data stays under that business’s control.

Keeping it safe

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information from misuse, loss and unauthorised access, and we keep it only as long as we need it for the purposes above.

Access, correction and complaints

You can ask to see or correct the personal information we hold about you, or raise a privacy concern, by emailing info@speedrunlab.ai. We will respond within a reasonable time. If you are not satisfied with our response, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
